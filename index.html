Our Story

Real Impact: Meet Mamie

Mamie is from the Kenema District in Sierra Leone. In the past, she and the people from her community used to fetch water from dirty lakes that had tadpoles, maggots, and other larvae, causing them to constantly be sick.

Thanks to charity: water, Mamie now has access to clean water, allowing her to cook and do the laundry without fear of contamination, allowing her to focus on her family and community.
